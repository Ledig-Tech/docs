---
title: "Limits"
description: "Distinguish conversion amounts, payout amounts and your account allowance."
---

A payment can be subject to more than one limit. Passing a conversion limit does not mean its payout is available.

| Limit | Amount to compare | Where to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conversion direction | `sourceAmount`, in the source currency | `GET /v1/liquidity/routes` |
| Payout method or network | Amount sent to the recipient, in the destination currency | `GET /v1/payouts/catalogue` and payment options |
| Account allowance | The payment's USD-equivalent value | Your dashboard account limits and returned allowance errors |

For example, a USDT-to-NGN conversion can fit the USDT conversion range while its NGN payout exceeds the selected bank method's maximum. That does not mean every NGN payment has the same limit.

## Conversion limits

Call `GET /v1/liquidity/routes` for the selected business. You can filter by `sourceCurrency`, `destinationCurrency`, or both. This read requires `view_rates` and access to quotes.

```http
GET /v1/liquidity/routes?sourceCurrency=USDT&destinationCurrency=NGN
```

Each returned direction has `sourceCurrency`, `destinationCurrency`, `minAmount` and `maxAmount`. Compare these bounds with the amount you convert, before fees. The reverse direction has its own limits; do not invert the rate to infer them.

Show `availability` and `status` alongside the bounds. Discovery does not reserve capacity or guarantee execution. Missing rows or failed reads do not mean unlimited conversion. Obtain a fresh [quote](/guides/quotes) before proceeding.

## Payout limits

Use `GET /v1/payouts/catalogue` to display published bands by currency, method and, for crypto, network. Each band's `minimumAmount` and `maximumAmount` apply to the amount sent to the recipient in that currency.

Keep separate bands separate. For example, bands of 10–100 and 200–500 do not permit 150. Do not combine them into a displayed limit of 10–500.

A returned `maximumAmount: null` means that band has no configured payout-route maximum. It does not remove account allowances or other eligibility checks. Missing data must not be displayed as unlimited.

A catalogue band can remain visible while sending is unavailable. Check `payoutAvailability`, then request `GET /v1/quotes/payment-options` for the recipient, destination currency, amount and exact crypto network where applicable. Use the returned `paymentMethodIds` value in the quote.

When you supply the selected `paymentMethodId` to payment options, the optional top-level `availability` can explain a rejection:

| `availability.reason` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `payout_amount_out_of_range` | The amount is outside this method or network's bounds. Any returned minimum and maximum use the destination currency. |
| `payout_amount_unavailable` | This amount is not in a supported band. Do not invent a continuous range. |
| `network_unavailable` | The selected network is unavailable for this request. |
| `recipient_incompatible` | The selected recipient does not match this payment option. |
| `payout_unavailable` | The payout is unavailable; no more specific customer reason is provided. |

## Account allowances

Account allowances are separate from currency and method limits. Your dashboard shows the applicable per-payment maximum, monthly allowance and remaining monthly allowance in USD equivalent. An allowance does not establish that a route is available or that your balance covers the total debit.

`PAYOUT_TRANSACTION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` can include `requestedUsd` and `limitUsd` in `details`. `PAYOUT_MONTHLY_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` can include `requestedUsd`, `remainingUsd`, `resetsAt` and `timezone: "UTC"`. Use those returned facts when present; do not calculate an authoritative remaining allowance in your client or invent missing amounts.

Where `INSTANT_CONVERSION_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` is returned, its `sourceCurrency` identifies a separate conversion allowance. Its `period`, `requested`, `remaining`, `limit` and UTC reset details must not be presented as a USD payout allowance.

See [Errors and troubleshooting](/errors/errors-and-troubleshooting) for example responses. Quotes and execution recheck the current rules, even if an earlier discovery request succeeded.
